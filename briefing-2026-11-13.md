**AI Briefing — Friday, November 13**

**Vapi launches Agent Loop, a self-improving harness for voice agents**
> Agent Loop builds, scores, and optimizes your voice agent on every call, closing the loop between production calls, evals, and prompt/config changes. It's in early access now with GA planned for early next year. Worth requesting access for Romey: it could replace manual review of receptionist calls and tuning after misbookings.
Source: [Vapi Blog](https://vapi.ai/blog/agent-loop-launch)

**Self-hosted Claude Managed Agents enters private beta**
> You can now run the Managed Agents harness, sandboxes, and session history in your own cloud or on-prem, and choose where requests to Claude are routed. Aimed at work that needs access to internal code and systems, like fixing broken builds or triaging alerts. Eligible orgs request access through their Anthropic account team.
Source: [Claude Devs](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2120938988165091693)

**Claude Meetings: Claude joins your video call and builds artifacts live**
> Claude joins as a visible participant. You can ask it questions out loud or in chat, and it builds docs, whiteboards, and other artifacts the team can watch and correct as they take shape. Afterward, notes, action items, the transcript, and everything it built land on the meeting's page. It's rolling out in beta on Team and Enterprise, starting with Google Meet.
Source: [Claude](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2120973994191094179)

**Anthropic on launching models and rethinking your harness on upgrade**
> New ClaudeDevs video on how Anthropic ships models and the API features that come with them, plus practical guidance on adapting your agent harness and measuring cost per task when you move to a new model. Directly relevant to anyone upgrading agents to Opus 5.5.
Source: [Claude Devs](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2120962481225146636)

**Grok Bot 1.0: AI teammates for the whole team**
> xAI shipped Grok Bot 1.0, positioned as persistent teammates that own outcomes. Bots can be shared, collaborate in Slack and Microsoft Teams, join meetings, and learn over time. xAI says it already uses Bots internally across accounting, support, sales, and marketing.
Source: [Grok Bot](https://x.com/bot/status/2120582957782200586)

**Grok Bot 1.0.1: meeting attendance, shared connectors, and approval gates for learning**
> On Team, Enterprise, and Ultra, a Bot can join a Google Meet, Zoom, or Teams call from a link, answer in the meeting chat, and send a summary afterward. Owners and Managers can share their own app login as a shared connector once an admin allows it. What teammates teach a Team Bot now waits in Review learnings until an owner or Manager approves it, which is a useful pattern for governing persistent agent memory. Admins can also restrict who shares Bots. Bots also stop sending empty "done" messages on turns with nothing to report.
Source: [Grok Bot Changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot#v1.0.1)

**Grok Bot on Mac gets native app access**
> Grok Bot on macOS can now use Messages, Mail, Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Location, and Contacts, extending it from cloud connectors into the local personal-data layer.
Source: [Grok Bot](https://x.com/bot/status/2120946968403095906)

**A Grok Bot orchestration setup worth copying**
> Greg Isenberg shares Rasmic's setup, which uses Grok Bot as an orchestrator that runs Claude Code for engineering and Paper for design. Key habits: give every serious project its own Bot, keep one general Bot for loose ideas, and send an idea at night to wake up to a working GitHub repo (one lazy prompt plus /goal got him five working builds). The full setup is linked in the thread.
Source: [Greg Isenberg](https://x.com/gregisenberg/status/2120594418231398677)

**The Home Repo pattern for agents across multiple repos**
> Matt Pocock published an article on the Home Repo, one central repo that holds your skills, steering files, and tooling so agents working across many repos share one source of truth. It includes a copy-pasteable prompt that sets the pattern up across your org's repos.
Source: [Matt Pocock](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2120838662254420341)

**/animatic: skills that turn PRs into explainer videos**
> Matt Pocock previewed two skills in progress: /setup-animatic picks and installs the tooling locally or on a remote machine, and /animatic generates an upload-ready video. The goal is to ship a short video with every important PR instead of walls of Markdown. Latency is the current bottleneck.
Source: [Matt Pocock](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2121211579098083668)

**Gemini 4 Hydrogen lands in the AI SDK and Cursor**
> Google's Gemini 4 Hydrogen is available through the AI SDK and in Cursor. At default effort it scores 54.3% on CursorBench, up from 39.6% for Gemini 3.8 Flash, priced at $1.50/M input and $7.50/M output tokens. It's a cheap option for sub-agents and high-volume tasks.
Source: [AI SDK](https://x.com/aisdk/status/2120974997456146699)
